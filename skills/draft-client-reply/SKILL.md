# Skill: Draft Client Reply

## Objective
Draft a clear, polite, and actionable reply to an incoming client inquiry.

## Format & Constraints
1. Acknowledge the client's main question or request in the first sentence.
2. Provide the direct answer or proposed next steps using short paragraphs or bullet points.
3. Keep the tone helpful, confident, and free of corporate jargon.
4. End with a clear call to action or confirmation of next steps.
